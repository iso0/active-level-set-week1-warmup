# FINAL_CLOSURE_DECISION

Development data only (old 405 cases). No new-pool label or input was accessed. Every number below is from the follow-up runs in `results/` or the previous package.

**A. Is the +0.095 oracle headroom attainable from currently available pre-query information?**
**NO IDENTIFIED SIGNAL.** Thirty-one allowed pre-query features (physics/geometry, revealed-label locality, M3/H/T/4-D-logistic uncertainty and disagreement, T leverage, coherent expected flips) and two cross-fitted predictors (leave-repeat-block-out ridge and small GBM) predict the candidate-level oracle value with median within-state Spearman ≤ 0.11, out-of-block R² < 0, top-decile AUROC ≈ 0.5; the predicted best candidate is worth +0.004–0.010 against +0.067 for the true best and −0.005 for the margin pick. The gate (ρ ≥ 0.30 and ≥ 2× enrichment) fails on both counts. The headroom is a training-subset sensitivity that only evaluation labels can steer.

**B. Is the oracle gain broad or mainly q20-specific?**
Broad in sign and class-neutral, concentrated in magnitude at the boundary: the q20-optimised oracle also gains +0.069 q30, +0.021 full-fold accuracy, +0.032 full-fold balanced accuracy and +0.05–0.12 KH recall without losing conduction recall. Case A restricted to the boundary region; not endpoint-specific overfitting alone.

**C. Are the persistent exceptions genuinely locally clustered after conditioning on campaign/class?**
No. The 13-row set is clustered only against an unconditioned null (p = 0.003); after class × campaign × configuration matching p = 0.14–0.59 (enrichment 1.3). Only the late-onset KH keep a marginal residual (nearest-exception distance p = 0.013, kNN p = 0.17), three of the seven being the alternative-configuration rows. Transient KH, depth-marginal C and the rest show no clustering. The structure is campaign/configuration composition.

**D. Does "more labels hurt M3" survive label-blind nested-subset tests?**
Yes, in a small, specific form: for band-first or margin sequences M3 peaks at n ≈ 80–120 and the all-label fit is worse by −0.016 q20 [−0.031, −0.004], −0.011 q30, −0.0045 full, −0.04 KH recall (no repeat block in the opposite direction). The decline comes from adding far-from-boundary rows last, not from local contradiction; random/campaign/maximin sequences do not show it. The oracle-path decline is mostly regression from an evaluation-selected optimum. "Poison" is withdrawn.

**E. Does M3R help?** No. Fixed-hyperparameter M3R changes < 0.1% of decisions on ordinary paths (Δ ≈ 0, Brier worse), reduces exception accuracy on all-label fits (0.141 → 0.097 at ε = 0.10), and erodes the oracle's gain (−0.016 … −0.023 q20 AULC, KH recall −0.04 … −0.06); re-optimised M3R is worse at every budget (q20 −0.003 … −0.006; the residual amplitude shrinks). **KILLED.**

**F. Does the one permitted local/nonstationary alternative help?** Not built: the clustering test (C) does not support within-campaign local structure, the oracle's valuable candidates are 86% ordinary rows with pool-average density and conflict, and campaign-aware discrepancy is inapplicable to a single-campaign pool. **CLOSED.**

**G. Does any NEW acquisition beat M3-margin on the old development gate?** No. No new acquisition was derived (A). The corrected-semantics coherent set-risk criteria (S2, S3) lose by 0.028–0.029; the tight-prior version by 0.011; M3+TV remains at +0.0025 [−0.003, +0.008] (F7) / −0.004 (tight) against the incumbent.

**H. Are Propositions 1–3 valid, and what is the exact safe wording?**
- Prop. 1: valid only for targets that determine Y_x. Safe wording: "For noiseless labels and any target that determines the queried label (the boundary, the excursion set, the full pool labelling), the one-step expected information gain equals the query's binary entropy, so maximum-uncertainty sampling is the information-optimal one-step rule (the generalised-binary-search fact; Dasgupta 2005, Nowak 2011). For the labels of other points the gain is H_b(π_x) − H(Y_x | Y_{−x}, D) ≤ H_b(π_x), and margin is not the maximiser." The slogan "margin is information-optimal for deterministic simulators" and "BALD/entropy criteria cannot beat margin" are withdrawn.
- Prop. 2: valid as written for posterior 0-1 / plug-in-set risks with any weights, any reference set and asymmetric costs (flip threshold moves); zero one-step reduction without a decision flip is the classical EVSI statement (Raiffa & Schlaifer 1961; Howard 1966), explicit for Bayes classification risk in MOCU (Yoon, Dougherty & Qian 2013; Zhao et al. 2021). It does not extend to Brier, log-loss, threshold-variance, Hausdorff or boundary-location losses. Safe wording: "an instantiation of the value-of-information zero-flip principle for finite-pool GPC set risk, with the bound r_u ≤ min(π_x, 1−π_x) for symmetric 0-1 loss"; not new.
- Prop. 3: valid as "coherent one-step reduction of the posterior 0-1 set risk under the Gaussian-Laplace posterior with stated observation semantics (S1 latent-sign query; S2 probit query, latent target; S3 probit query, label target), computed with exact bivariate-normal orthant probabilities". "Exact Bayes-optimal transductive rule" is withdrawn.

**I. Does the exact/coherent XSUR negative result survive the corrected probabilistic semantics?** Yes. S2: −0.028 [−0.041, −0.015]; S3: −0.029 [−0.042, −0.016] (q20 AULC 16–40 vs M3-margin, 100 runs); previous S1: −0.028. Same selection behaviour under all three. Retained, with corrected wording.
