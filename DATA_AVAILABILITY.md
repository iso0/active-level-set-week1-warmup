# Data and execution-evidence availability

The Research Hub publishes original reviewed text/code and a selected owner-authorized synthetic evidence collection: numerical CSV/JSON results, frozen configurations and ten original PNG figures. See the [curated index](docs/research/CURATED_EVIDENCE_INDEX.md) and [public provenance/checksum manifest](docs/research/CURATED_EVIDENCE_MANIFEST.csv).

This extension adds no raw simulation dataset, compressed unit archive, NPZ checkpoint, SQLite database, TUM input, simulator internal or protected evaluation target. Embedded source-relative raw-record references preserve provenance; they do not make those records downloadable.

The complete execution archive remains in offline preservation and will not be mirrored wholesale to Releases, LFS or cloud storage. PENDING_ARCHIVE/RESTRICTED entries outside the selected published subset remain unavailable and do not imply upload promises or redistribution permission.

Scientific values, statuses, missing outcomes, failed screens and reported conclusions were retained without recomputation. Synthetic monitor results, historical Track A/M3, binary G3 tasks, OLD depth-threshold findings, later C1C in-pool evidence and external physical validation retain distinct scopes. Publication is not new validation.

Private path/worker metadata was sanitized only in publication derivatives. Original/published hashes and exact field-level transformations are available in the public manifest and transformation log. Original sources, frozen originals and preservation backups were not modified.
