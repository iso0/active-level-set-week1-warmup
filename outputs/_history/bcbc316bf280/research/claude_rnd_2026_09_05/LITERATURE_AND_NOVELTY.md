## 2. Mathematical overlap, stated plainly

- The threshold-surface model T (MATHEMATICAL_DEVELOPMENT eq. 4–5) is a special case of Keeley et al. 2023 (constant slope) and, for fixed hyperparameters, an ordinary probit GPC with a known-mean, context-only kernel (Chu & Ghahramani-type). It is **not a new model family**; the previous review already said so.
- The set-directed acquisition (8) is Letham et al.'s GlobalSUR on a finite reference set; (9) is a variance-reduction criterion of the Picheny/Bect/Chevalier family. Neither is new.
- The finite-pool 0-1 risk (1) with q=p is Roy–McCallum / MOCU; with q=π it is the Bect–Letham misclassification risk.
- The censored-depth model is a Tobit GP; using its level set as the boundary has no located precedent in an active-learning context, but the combination was killed on development data and is not claimed.

## 3. What is defensibly new (conditional on the development results in OLD_DATA_RND_REPORT.md)

1. **A mechanism result for physics-mean + capped-residual GPCs (M3-type)**: when the latent scale is set by a near-unregularised logistic slope and the residual amplitude is bounded, the Laplace latent variance is nearly constant across candidates and small relative to |m|, so every acquisition of the form g(|m|, v, C) — straddle, EMI, local variance reduction, p(1−p) SUR — is monotone in margin (R1–R3). The committed ranking correlations (0.977–0.999) and the 1.18B null are the empirical confirmation. No paper in the search states this collapse for hybrid physics/GP classifiers, although it is elementary.
2. **A ceiling decomposition for the benchmark**: the near-boundary endpoint's data-determined ceiling (0.8565 for M3 with all labels; 0.83–0.86 for nine model classes and an auxiliary-output model) is produced by 13 identifiable label-definition exception cases, and the M3-margin curve reaches it by B60; this yields a quantitative zero-headroom bound over B41–80 that explains the chain of published nulls. This is an application/diagnostic contribution, not a methodological one, but it is falsifiable on the new pool (SATURATION_PREDICTIONS.md).
3. **The negative model results themselves**: boundary-unit posteriors (T family, six prior settings) and continuous-output augmentation (CDL) are *worse* than the rigid physics step at B16–B32 on identical revealed labels, converging to it by B48. The early window is where headroom exists, and it is where a rigid, near-hard 1-D physics step is the best available predictor. This constrains the whole "represent boundary uncertainty" programme on this benchmark.
4. **A frozen, blinded, crossed model×acquisition external test** with an early-window primary endpoint chosen by an old-data power analysis, and a label-free feasibility gate. This is methodology of evaluation, not a new estimator.

No priority claim is made for "GP on the boundary", "continuous GP active learning", "mixed binary/depth likelihood", or "finite-pool SUR for GPC".
