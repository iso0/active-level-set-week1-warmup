estimation; rare category detection and active search; cold-start/initial-pool selection; k-center and
covering-based active learning; monotone/active monotone classification; dominance-based rough sets;

**Bottom line.** No single result is a strong standalone novelty claim. The defensible contribution is
the *integrated, assumption-explicit framework* for rare-regime finite-pool level-set estimation
(items 1–4, 10–14), with honest real-data tests, plus two evaluation findings (items 11, 13) that
appear not to be standard in the active-learning benchmarking literature. Items 6, 9 and 14 must be
presented as known.
