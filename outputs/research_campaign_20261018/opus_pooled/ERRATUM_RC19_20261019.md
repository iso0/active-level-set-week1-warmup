# Erratum to RC19 (dated 2026-10-19, RC19B continuation)

Source: the owner's independent audit of commit ae7ca34e. Every finding was reproduced against the full-precision unit caches (`opus_rc19b/repair/`, `opus_rc19b/export/EXPORT_ACCEPTANCE.json`). RC19 files are kept unchanged. Corrected tables and exports are new versions.

## What changes

**A. Confirmation expenditure was misreported.**
- RC19's endpoint keeps `confirmed = b`, the first budget with the level at b and at b + 4, and `confirm_spend = b + 4`. The report and `FINAL_REPORT_RC19.md` printed `confirmed` as if it were the number of simulations spent.
- The actual confirmation spend (total revealed simulations, startup included) is:
  - region, SHQ + STRADDLE: **27.125** (range 25–30), not 23.1 (21–26);
  - label: **PHYSCF_ATS 17.875, E1 + straddle 23.750, G3 28.250**, not 13.9 / 19.8 / 24.3.
- The same +4 shift applies to every regression per-output and joint table.
- First-qualifying budgets (the first recorded budget at the level) are now reported separately.
- The paired differences are unchanged, e.g. PHYSCF_ATS − E1 = −5.875 (6 fewer, 1 equal, 1 more).
- Corrected tables: `opus_rc19b/repair/{region,label,reg}_endpoints_v2.csv` and `REPAIR_endpoints.md`.
- A held-out target is an offline benchmark, not a deployable stopping rule.

**B. Prediction exports were lossy.**
- RC19's `report/predictions_label.npz` and `predictions_region.npz` stored float16. Metrics recomputed from them differ from the evaluator:
  - label BA in 44 of 2,968 cells, max |Δ| 0.0349 (worst cell G3, repeat 8, B44: 0.855 vs 0.890);
  - label log loss by up to 0.0659;
  - region BA in 37 of 3,392 cells, max |Δ| 0.0022.
- The regression export was float32, but only at 6 budgets.
- **The RC19 CSV metrics are not affected:** they were computed from the float32 unit caches.
- Replacement: `opus_rc19b/export/predictions_{label,region,reg}_f32.npz`, float32 at every recorded budget, with test IDs and budgets per unit.
- Recomputing every metric cell from them reproduces the RC19 CSVs:
  - label: 2,968 cells (BA, both recalls, AUC, Brier, log loss, q20 BA, short-K, batch diagnostics);
  - region: 3,392 cells;
  - regression: 8,512 cells (R², cov90, NLPD).
  - Max |Δ| ≤ 4e-15; status PASS.
- The old files are superseded but kept.

**C. "Cross-fitted (leave-one-out)" overstated what PHYSCF did.**
- RC19's PHYSCF features are the closed-form leave-one-out predictions of the GP fitted on **all** paid runs. The fit's hyperparameters (shared lengthscales, signal and noise variances) and the response normalisation (mean and SD of the paid outputs, including the held-out run) were therefore not refitted without that run.
- This is an **approximation** of cross-fitting, not full cross-fitting.
- No unpaid or test outcome entered; the shadow validation still holds.
- RC19B keeps the implementation as `ATSAPPROX` (the historical approximate control) and adds the honest K-fold version `ATSCF`. In ATSCF the held-out paid runs are excluded from the upstream GP, the shared-lengthscale fit, the normalisation and any depth threshold.

**D. Joint 8-output completion was summarised incompletely.**
- `FINAL_REPORT_RC19.md`, the ledger row RC19-RESULTS and the memory note named only SHQ + RANDOM (7/8) and M0Q + MOIVR (5/8).
- Complete list: SHQ + RANDOM **7/8**, M0Q + MOIVR **5/8**, M0Q + RANDOM **2/8**, SHQ + MOIVR **1/8**, every MAXIMIN arm **0/8**.
- `RESULTS_TABLE.md` itself listed all arms.

**E. Freeze scope stated more strongly than it was.**
- RC19's manifest made workers hash-check the learner, task, evaluator (`rc19_eval.py`) and data files.
- The report generator `rc19_report.py` was committed in the freeze commit 8f82e404, before any final result, and was unchanged up to ae7ca34e. Workers never hash-checked it.
- `rc19_export.py`, `rc19_figures.py`, `rc19_viewer.py` and `rc19_ax_report.py` were written after the final runs.
- The RC19 protocol sentence "the report generator … is part of the freeze" refers to the commit, not to a runtime check.
- RC19B's manifest hashes the evaluation, report and export code.

**F. The RC19 3D view mixes learners.**
- Each dot is the out-of-fold prediction of the fold in which that run was a test run. The five folds are five different learners, each trained on a different paid set.
- Choosing a fold changes only the paid markers, not the model behind every point.
- RC19B adds a single-learner view in which every prediction and every paid point come from one fold's learner.
