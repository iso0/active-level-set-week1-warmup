## 1. Question and hypotheses

**Main question.** By learning a better model from the simulations' physical outputs, can a physical quantity, a physically defined region, or the regime label be learned to the same accuracy with fewer simulations?

**H-MODEL.** The earlier selectors all used one representation: a stationary homoskedastic GP, fitted per output and per campaign. The proposed model adds three things:
1. transfer from the historical campaign;
2. shared hyperparameters across the outputs of the same simulations;
3. a QA-aware observation model.

H-MODEL states that this model reaches the target accuracy with fewer NEW simulations. This is a hypothesis; a richer model is not assumed to be better.

**H-ACQ.** Given a model, adaptive acquisition adds simulations saved beyond the space-filling design. The model effect and the acquisition effect are measured separately.

**What the learner may use:**
- x for every run;
- for paid runs: their outputs, their QA metadata (record length) and their label;

**What the learner never receives:**
- unpaid or test outputs or labels;

- held-out monitors.

Predicted auxiliary outputs are functions of x, not measurements.

## 3. Models (one engine: `src/rc18_gp.py`, validated against sklearn)

**Common structure.** All models use standardised targets ys = (y − m0(x) − μ)/σ over the paid rows, with:

  ys = f(z) + ε,  f ~ GP(0, s_f² · Matérn3/2_ARD(z, z′; ℓ)),  ε_i ~ N(0, s_n² · w_i).

**Inputs.** z are the fold-standardised log inputs (log P, log VX, log LS, log ST).

**Fitting.** ML-II by L-BFGS-B with analytic gradients, starting from s_f² = 1, ℓ = 1, s_n² = 0.1 (bounds as in PHYS_AL).

**Predictions.** Latent mean, latent variance and observation variance; the noise is never added twice (fix A1).

| Model | m0(x) | Lengthscales | w_i |
|---|---|---|---|
| **M0** (simple; = PHYS_AL's GP) | 0 | per output, ML-II | 1 |
| **M0Q** | 0 | per output | 10⁴ if the paid run's record ended before 0.95 × its planned end TE, else 1 |
| **SHQ** | 0 | shared across all 8 outputs: ML-II of the summed marginal likelihoods, with per-output s_f², s_n²; then each output on its own paid rows | as M0Q |
| **TRQ** (NEW) | μ_OLD(x): a GP (M0 form) fitted once on all OLD runs per target | per output (residual GP) | as M0Q |
| **TRSQ** (NEW; RICH) | μ_OLD(x) | shared across the 8 residual outputs | as M0Q |

- **Small samples:** the transfer models with fewer than 5 usable paid rows use an offset only, μ_OLD + mean residual.
- **RICH models:** NEW → TRSQ; OLD → SHQ (OLD has no earlier campaign).
- **What the Q rule does:** a truncated record is still paid and evaluated, and keeps its label. It only enters its own regression row with 10⁴ × noise, which is close to "no regression row", as in Astra's data contract.

## 4. Designs and acquisitions

**Regression designs:**
- **MAXIMIN** (all models). Predictions start at the first budget the model can fit: b ≥ 3 for M0-type models and b ≥ 0 for transfer models.
- **RANDOM** and **MOIVR** (M0 and RICH), from the common 8-point start to B60.
- **MOIVR** = argmax over unpaid pool runs of Σ_t IVR_t(x)/Var(paid y_t), summed over the population's primary targets. IVR is the closed-form integrated latent variance reduction over the fold pool. One simulation reveals all outputs.

**Region** — level set {x: velocity spike log-ratio ≥ τ = log 2}, i.e. the 99.9th percentile of melt velocity is above twice its median:
- **Models:** {M0, RICH}.
- **Acquisitions:**
  - MAXIMIN; RANDOM;
  - STRADDLE (Bryan et al. 2006): 1.96 σ − |μ − τ|;
  - TRUVAR (Bogunovic et al. 2016, LSE form): total latent variance reduction over the unclassified set {|μ − τ| < 1.96 σ}, η = 0, with STRADDLE as the fallback when that set is empty;
  - ASTRA_SURPRISE and ASTRA_STRADDLE_GLOBAL: Astra's frozen `discovery.SurpriseLocalDiscovery` through its ask/tell interface, with the same global schedule (every 4th query global-random), the local reaction on or off, latent and noise SD passed separately, inputs scaled to the pool's unit box, `Config(threshold = τ)`.
- **Budget:** B8–B40.

**Keyhole label (NEW):**
- **Arms:** G3_MARGIN (frozen Week 18 G3 + margin); ACTIVE_E1_STRADDLE (frozen Week 18 E1: GPR on log A plus a paid-pair threshold); ACTIVE_E1_MAXIMIN; TR_E1_STRADDLE (the same threshold rule and straddle, with depth model TRQ); TR_E1_MAXIMIN.
- **Budget:** B8–B40.
- **One-class rule (as TRAINED_AL):** while the usable paid labels have one class, every arm follows the label-free path, and G3 predicts the paid class.

## 5. Endpoints and verdicts

**Regression** (per population × model × design × target × repeat; test predictions pooled over the 5 folds of the repeat):
- R²(b) = 1 − SSE/SSE_ref, with the population-mean reference;
- RMSE, 90 % observation coverage, NLPD;
- R²-AULC over B8–B60;
- sustained budget-to-target on the grid {0, 4, …, 56}: R² ≥ level at b and at b + 4, censored at 64;
- level_t = 0.9 × R²_full(M0), where R²_full(M0) is M0 with the whole fold pool paid, averaged over the final repeats.

**PRIMARY (one comparison).** NEW, regression, model effect under the identical MAXIMIN design, **RICH (TRSQ) vs M0**.
- Endpoint: queries saved, S(t, r) = B_M0(t, r) − B_RICH(t, r), over the 6 NEW primary targets and the 4 final repeats.
- **Verdict FEWER SIMULATIONS (DEV)** iff all three hold:
  1. mean S ≥ 8 queries;
  2. the per-repeat mean S_r > 0 in all 4 repeats;
  3. no target has mean S_t < −4.
- Otherwise: **NOT ESTABLISHED**.
- The R²-AULC difference (mean over targets and repeats) is reported next to the verdict.

**EXPLORATORY** (no correction and no superiority claim; reported with all 4 per-repeat paired values, their mean and range):
- **E1 decomposition** (NEW): M0Q − M0 (QA), SHQ − M0Q (sharing), TRQ − M0Q (transfer), TRSQ − TRQ (sharing given transfer).
- **E2 (OLD):** SHQ vs M0, with the same endpoints.
- **E3 acquisition given the model:** MOIVR − MAXIMIN and RANDOM − MAXIMIN, for M0 and for RICH.
- **E4 calibration:** coverage and NLPD at B8 and B24.
- **E5 region:**
  - learning: BA(b) of (μ ≥ τ) vs truth on test, both recalls, BA-AULC over B8–B40, sustained budget-to-target BA ≥ 0.95 × BA_full(M0) on {8, …, 36}, censored at 44;
  - collection: share of paid runs inside the band |y − τ| ≤ 0.25, and share of the pool's minority side paid by B40;
  - contrasts: (a) same model, STRADDLE − MAXIMIN and TRUVAR − STRADDLE; (b) same acquisition, RICH − M0 under STRADDLE; (c) same global schedule, ASTRA_SURPRISE − ASTRA_STRADDLE_GLOBAL; (d) collection vs learning.
- **E6 label (NEW):**
  - metrics: BA at p ≥ 0.5, sensitivity, specificity, q20, short-K captured/total, Brier, log loss, AUC;
  - contrasts: TR_E1_STRADDLE − ACTIVE_E1_STRADDLE, each vs G3_MARGIN, and the MAXIMIN design controls.
- **E7 temporal phase model:** separate section 8, frozen before its final run.

**Never-reached and false confidence:**
- Arms that never reach a level are reported as censored (64 or 44), never as a hit.
- "False confidence" is reported as the share of test runs outside the 90 % interval at the budget where an arm first claims the level.

- The model directions were chosen from an all-DEV data inspection (RC18-B0).

  - Label: every arm has BA 0.65–0.73.
  - The primary bar (≥ 8 queries saved, all repeats positive) was set with these numbers known. It is a replication test of the development effect on new partitions of the same simulations, not an independent test.
