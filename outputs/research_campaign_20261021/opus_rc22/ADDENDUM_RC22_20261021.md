# Addendum to RC22 (2026-10-21, after review) — interpretation only; frozen RC22 numbers unchanged

**A. HA_LR is not free of temperature and spike information.**
- What the code does: `src/rc22_common.py::state` computes HA_LR from `A.cf(Z, rev, A.ATS, "SHQ", rows, A.KF)`, the upstream shared over (log A, T, S). Its lengthscales and quality weights are learned from all three paid channels; HA_LR then uses only the depth column in the final classifier.
- What RC22 tested: removing T and S from the FINAL DECISION LAYER only, not from the whole learning system.
- Verified in RC23 (`opus_rc23/RC23_VALIDATION.json`): changing the paid T / S values changes HA_LR's probabilities by up to 0.53.
- Withdrawn statements (FINAL_REPORT_RC22.md, claims C73, research log): "HA_LR does not need the temperature / spike emulators" and "it is naturally immune to their missingness".
- Corrected wording: "HA_LR drops T and S from the classifier; its depth predictions still come from the upstream shared with T and S."
- RC23 tests the fully lean system.

**B. "8 simulations fewer to BA ≥ 0.95" is a restricted score of a secondary target.**
- Attainment: HA_LR + margin confirmed BA ≥ 0.95 in 8/8 fold-pooled repeats; PHR_NRMAP + margin in 7/8.
- Observed difference: on the seven repeats where both confirmed, the mean is −8.14 simulations (per repeat −1, −10, −16, 0, −12, 0, −18).
- Restricted score: the reported −8.0 assigns 64 to the non-attaining reference.
- 64 is not a strict lower bound. Budgets are recorded at every integer and confirmation needs b and b + 4 ≤ 60. A trajectory that was not confirmed by B60 could at best confirm at b = 57, with spend 61. So the smallest spend it could still have had is 61, not 64.
- From RC23 on: observed both-attained differences, attainment counts and a restricted score (censored = 61) are reported separately.
