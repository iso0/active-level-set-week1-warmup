# Erratum to RC21 / RC21B (2026-10-21) — wording only; no number changes

Source: Astra's independent read-only context audit `OPUS_CONTEXT_AUDIT_20261021.md` (package
`astra_representation_planning_20261020`, outside this repository). The three points are correct and are adopted. The frozen
RC21 / RC21B results, tables and verdicts stay as recorded.

1. **The corner is not shown to be unlearnable.** FINAL_REPORT_RC21.md (Turkish summary and §6), PROTOCOL_RC21_STAGE1.md §1,
   claims C67 and the research log say or imply that "no input-smooth model can separate" the fast-scan corner because
   nearest-neighbour label agreement there is 0.44 (0.91 in the pool). That is too strong. The statistic shows difficulty at that
   Euclidean metric and sample density; for finite distinct inputs, smooth functions can interpolate any labelling, so it is not an
   impossibility proof. It is consistent with limited resolution, misspecification, annotation/monitor differences or missing
   information, and does not distinguish them. Corrected wording: "The corner is label-interleaved at the available density;
   the tested models did not resolve it."
2. **β_h is not likelihood-identified.** MODEL_DEFINITIONS_RC21.md says "β_h is the only term that varies along d at fixed U"
   and src/rc21_phr.py says β_h "stays identified". The physical-summary predictions F̃_t(Z) can also vary along the h direction,
   so the likelihood depends on combinations such as β_h + w_1 when F̃_1 is close to z_h. Corrected wording: "The transverse
   residual cannot duplicate the explicit h trend; the h coefficient is regularised by its prior, not identified separately from
   the physical-summary terms."
3. **Targets were applied to fold-pooled curves.** RC21 / RC21B spend and attainment tables (src/rc21_report.py with
   src/rc19_eval.label_metrics) apply each target to the curve that pools the five folds' out-of-fold test predictions within a
   repeat. "8/8" therefore counts repeats whose pooled curve met the target, not individual learners. RC22 reports both.
