Week 9 Phase 2.1R — interactive 3D process-space views

The process space has four inputs, so no single 3D picture can show it. These four views each drop one input from the axes and keep it as a colour mode and hover field, so every input gets a turn being the hidden one. All 405 frozen simulations appear in every view (73 Keyhole), coloured by the manual has_keyhole label by default.

P · VX · LS
ST dropped

Positions come from P, VX and LS. ST (substrate temperature) stays available as a colour mode and in the hover card.
P · VX · ST
LS dropped

Positions come from P, VX and ST. LS (laser spot radius) stays available as a colour mode and in the hover card.
P · LS · ST
VX dropped

Positions come from P, LS and ST. VX (scan speed) stays available as a colour mode and in the hover card.
VX · LS · ST
P dropped

Positions come from VX, LS and ST. P (laser power) stays available as a colour mode and in the hover card.
Each view is self-contained: no libraries, no network, works offline. Colour by label, by log h, or by the dropped input; filter to Keyhole only, Conduction only, or to the simulations with a usable width trace; drag to rotate and scroll to zoom.
