Given a latent eta function, pi=integral eta dmu and w_m(pi)=1-pi^m-(1-pi)^m. Integrating the other m-1 IID examples gives coefficients A_m(x)=E[w_m(pi) eta(x)/pi] and B_m(x)=E[w_m(pi)(1-eta(x))/(1-pi)]. These are bounded by m, even for a new prediction point, and include the dependence between eta and pi. Use d=1 iff A_m>=B_m. The decision-dependent Bayes risk is 1/2 integral min(A_m,B_m)dmu; the action-independent zero-class term is 1/2 E[1-w_m]. The latter cancels in expected information value by the tower property. This is the only primary decision/acquisition construction.
