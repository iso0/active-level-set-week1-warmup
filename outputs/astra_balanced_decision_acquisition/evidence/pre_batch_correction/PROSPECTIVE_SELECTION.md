**Prospective selection — 14 October 2026, before any new numerical comparison**

Select one method: posterior balanced-risk decisions and paired-output expected balanced-risk reduction (PBR). Keep V2's complete mixture, fixed priors and EP/fallback policy. This is expected-error reduction with an uncertain, model-integrated population class weight; neither a new GP family nor a novelty claim for Bayes decisions/SUR.

Target: canonical recorded-label prediction under a declared feature distribution mu. In the controlled experiment mu is the uniform empirical measure on the fixed feature-only candidate pool. Its role is a training-only quadrature approximation to the feature distribution of independent evaluation cases. It does NOT become the paid-sample distribution as sampling proceeds. Predictive probabilities at paid inputs remain probabilities of an independent recorded-label prediction, not replacements for their observed labels. This population objective is a deliberate inductive proxy for the repository's pooled held-out BA, NOT the exact posterior expectation of BA on its fixed, already recorded test labels. Reference-distribution error is part of the experiment. No true prevalence, held-out features/outcomes, short-K/q20 membership or post-hoc VX cutoff enters the learner.

For a posterior draw eta, pi=sum(v_i eta_i); loss is half of the sum of the population false-negative and false-positive rates. Decisions compare E[eta(x)/pi] with E[(1-eta(x))/(1-pi)], retaining dependence. The primary score is the expected reduction in this same optimized loss after future available depth and label. No local-retention guarantee is claimed. A finite-recorded-pool alternative is derived and checked separately, with paid labels fixed, both-class events explicit, and a constant loss of 1/2 on zero-class events. It is NOT silently substituted for the population objective.

Forecasts: no numeric superiority forecast. The exact Bayes action cannot have higher risk under its own correct posterior/loss than plug-in or 0.5 actions. This says nothing unconditional about empirical BA under misspecification. Withdrawal of a harmful bridge is already V2's contribution. Correct class weights may improve negative recall while reducing positive/constructed-region recall. Correctly calibrated probabilities can still sacrifice a locally mixed positive region. No universal acquisition ordering is expected; V2's earlier paired variance selector won BA-AULC in only 2/9 cases.

Controlled comparisons: seven analytic, mostly misspecified four-input worlds (informative depth, redundant depth, harmful depth, rare negatives, locally mixed region, covariate-selected startup, missing depth); four seeds 5101–5104. Twenty-four query candidates, 96 independent evaluator cases, same hidden outcomes and feature-only B6 startup for three selectors. B6 through B10 inclusive, four queries. All three selectors use identical V2 inference, information, reference measure and PBR decisions: PBR expected-risk reduction; original V2 probability-variance reduction; PBR-normalized decision margin. Historical 0.5, paid-fraction, and posterior-mean-prevalence rules are decision-only readouts on identical fitted probabilities; they do not choose queries. No historical 0.5-margin sequential arm. No simulator or real-data fit.

Metrics at every budget: held-out BA, both recalls, Brier, log loss, probability MSE, 90% probability coverage/width, constructed-region error and positive recall, model risk, reference prevalence, paid fraction, paths and numerical diagnostics. Region membership is evaluator-only. Primary acquisition contrasts: paired BA-AULC B6–B10 and terminal BA, per world and seed. Pooling unlike worlds is descriptive only. Undefined one-class metrics remain null. No query-saving endpoint is registered; path divergence is not saving. All stops/losses remain in denominators and archives; no seed replacement.

Numerics: stratified randomized Sobol normals, four independent scrambles; future depth conditioned analytically within each Gaussian component, mixture masses reweighted by predictive depth density; both future labels summed exactly through likelihood-weighted moments. No importance weighting by the narrow response likelihood. ESS is assessed for future-label weights, including component masses. Raw risk is obtained from the same quadrature table before/after observation (Jensen nonnegativity); tower discrepancies against unconditional decision coefficients are reported. This is exact conditioning of an approximate EP posterior, not exact inference; actual next-step EP refits can disagree with lookahead.

Acquisition ladder, chosen only by numerical diagnostics: Sobol 2^8 / GH9, 2^10 / GH17, 2^12 / GH33, four scrambles each. Accept order difference <=0.002 and four-scramble 2-SE <=0.002; compare score gaps against those errors. Indifference band 0.002; deterministic lowest candidate ID within the numerical near-tie set. Stop if unresolved at ceiling. An outcome-weighted mass <=0.002 may have ESS below 32; all branches are still integrated. Margin uses the same estimated action coefficients, normalized by their sum. Its numerical ambiguity is logged. Original variance uses GH15/CDF48 and GH31/CDF64, then GH63/CDF96 if necessary; tolerance 0.0002 and deterministic near ties. No setting is selected on scientific scores.

Decision integration: four Sobol scrambles, 2^10 draws/component, then 2^12; escalate to 2^14 if max normalized-score change >0.015 or pi change >0.003. A zero within numerical error is reported as unresolved, deterministic positive tie. Finite log-tail arithmetic avoids replacing pi with a known class count. Coefficients at reference atoms are bounded by 1/v_i. No empirical threshold search.
