# Licensing scope — Research Hub v1

The repository owner iso0 expressly confirmed ownership of the four original Astra research campaigns and authorized this reviewed publication on 2026-10-28.

## Scope is file-specific

[LICENSE_SCOPE.csv](LICENSE_SCOPE.csv) is the authoritative allowlist of files covered by this publication. Every path in that manifest is part of the imported Research Hub or its approved licensing/navigation additions. Paths absent from it retain their existing terms.

- Original imported Python code, research checks/software implementations and authored requirements specifications: **MIT**. See the [complete MIT License](licenses/LICENSE-MIT.txt), verified against the [Open Source Initiative text](https://opensource.org/license/mit).
- Original research documents, protocols, explanations and editorial Hub documentation/metadata: **CC BY 4.0**, only to the extent of the owner's original expression. See [scope and attribution guidance](licenses/CC-BY-4.0.md), the [official license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) and [legal code](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.en).
- The standard license notice itself is identified as legal-license text; its reproduction does not assert original research authorship of legal wording.

No repository-root license was added. This document does not grant a new license over existing thesis files outside this explicit import. In particular, canonical existing thesis sources/reports linked at the pinned base commit retain their original terms even when a historical context copy occurs inside the Hub.

## Exclusions

Third-party dependency implementations, external datasets, attributed quotations, figures, linked literature, trademarks and other rights not owned by iso0 are excluded from the new grant. Dependency names/version lists do not relicense the packages they describe. Existing third-party notices must be retained; none were removed.

The RAW campaign entries are PENDING_ARCHIVE, not authorized data releases. Other withheld files remain RESTRICTED. No new raw unit records, simulation datasets, checkpoints, databases, figures or protected evaluation targets are included in this publication.

## Provenance and changes

[Public provenance](PROVENANCE_PUBLIC.json) retains original repo IDs, commits, blob identities and hashes. The 221 included original reading sources retain their scientific conclusions; the original public-draft code and source payloads remain unchanged. Eighteen source-document reading views already contained navigation-only changes before this publication. Editorial permission/status/license pages were updated after owner approval; scientific endpoints were not recomputed.
