## Scope and availability

Synthetic latent-monitor results are separate from historical Track A/M3, Week 17-18 G3 binary endpoints, OLD depth-threshold tasks, later C1C in-pool recommendations and external physical validation. Negative findings, partial paths, missing-outcome bounds, practical gates, family harms and cost exclusions remain unchanged. Embedded source-relative raw-record paths are provenance references, not promises that those records are public. The complete execution archive remains offline; no Release, LFS migration or complete archive upload is provided.

## Reading the figures

AD18's paid-simulations axis denotes synthetic-monitor queries, not physical SPH runs. Its curve bands are descriptive. RP20's complete-world intervals must be read with the saved missing-outcome bounds and three failed-path records; its first-hit plot is search evidence, not learned boundary accuracy. LC24 remains DEV on reused worlds. JL26's learning curves retain two dependent paths per world and descriptive family means. No image pixels or numerical plot values were edited.
