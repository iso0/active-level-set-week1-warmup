# Claim ledger

One consolidated ledger of what the thesis may and may not claim. It starts from the Week 8 consolidation ledger (`outputs/week8_02_thesis_consolidation/final_thesis_claim_ledger.csv`, C01 to C13), corrects it with the frozen Week 8.5 decisions, the H=320 closure, the Phase 1.5 red team (`reports/05_final_red_team_report.md`) and the Week 9 per-phase `claim_ledger.md` files, and adds C14 to C40. Every path is relative to the archive root `../thesis_work_chatgpt`. Every number is copied from the artifact named in the same row.

Precedence rule. When wordings conflict, the later frozen result wins. Week 8.5 (`outputs/week8_5_frozen_confirmation/decision_ledger.csv`) overrides the Week 8 consolidation. The H=320 closure (`outputs/week9_phase1_close_week8/query_saving_claim_decision.json`) is a post hoc diagnostic: it narrows wording but does not upgrade or downgrade a Week 8.5 decision. Week 9 phase ledgers override earlier phase recommendations. Every override is listed in the last table. Method of record: `binary_margin` on a 4D Matern-3/2 GPC (confirmatory); M3 hybrid surrogate (exploratory).

## Status vocabulary

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| SUPPORTED confirmatory | passed a preregistered rule under the frozen Week 8.5 protocol (100 matched runs, 3000 Random continuations), or is a frozen data fact |
| SUPPORTED exploratory | supported by a predeclared contrast in an exploratory phase (Phase 6/7 20-run protocol, or Week 9 replay on the frozen A0 path); cite as evidence, not confirmation |
| QUALIFIED | favourable point estimate, but a preregistered gate failed or the interval crosses zero; the qualification travels with the number |
| NOT SUPPORTED | tested and not established, or explicitly rejected |
| PROHIBITED | must not appear in any form |
