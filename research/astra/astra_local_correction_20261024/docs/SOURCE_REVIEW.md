The previous Astra code used a stationary compact Wendland covariance,
`(1-r)_+^5 (5r+1)` in four dimensions, with local SD 0.6 and radii 0.45/0.9.
Its marginal variance was 0.36 at every input. The strong learned-ARD comparator
improved common-prefix predictions, while the proposed local bank was largely
suppressed. This motivates, but does not prove, a global evidence penalty.
New controls preserve this compact shape and its two scales, transplanting them
onto the same strong learned GP. This is a controlled structural comparison,
not a rerun of the historical weaker finite mixture.

## Primary literature and nearest methods

- Ba and Joseph (2012), [Composite Gaussian process models (PENDING_ARCHIVE)](../../../../docs/research/ARCHIVE_MANIFEST.md#pending-13a8608413fffee7),
  section 3.2, equations 11–13: global plus local GP with spatially varying local
  variance already exists. Its covariance is D K_local D, not a variance added
  to only one side of a covariance matrix. The original work estimates variance
  structure and targets function prediction. A gate alone is not novelty.
- Davis, Hans and Santner (2021), [Bayesian composite GP (PENDING_ARCHIVE)](../../../../docs/research/ARCHIVE_MANIFEST.md#pending-d31211c2970b1ae1),
  sections 2–3: stochastic variance structure and posterior parameter integration
  extend composite GPs. Our finite gate bank with plug-in global parameters is
  much more restricted; it does not reproduce their full hierarchical posterior.
- Gramacy and Lee (2008), [Treed GP (PENDING_ARCHIVE)](../../../../docs/research/ARCHIVE_MANIFEST.md#pending-86dcaa214cfce02b), section 4:
  Bayesian partition averaging is a distinct established way to localize model
  flexibility. We preserve continuity within each gated component and integrate
  a finite location prior, rather than claim a new nonstationary GP principle.
- Letham et al. (2022), [Bernoulli level-set lookahead (PENDING_ARCHIVE)](../../../../docs/research/ARCHIVE_MANIFEST.md#pending-630ade4149e1e550):
  global expected classification-loss reduction is established. Its Bernoulli
  observation law differs from this continuous-monitor pilot. A more expensive
  lookahead is not justified before matched-data prediction improves.
- Ozbayram, Olivier and Graham-Brady (2026), [Heteroscedastic GP active learning (PENDING_ARCHIVE)](../../../../docs/research/ARCHIVE_MANIFEST.md#pending-258cc53d3a5aa87c):
  current primary work distinguishes noise estimation and active-learning
  behavior. We inspected the publisher's available model/abstract sections, not
  its inaccessible full equations or implementation. It reinforces the need for
  noise-only controls; we do not claim to outperform that method.

The possible contribution is a bounded mechanism discriminator and a useful
conditional early-learning advantage, if established. No general novelty,
optimality, sample-complexity or physical-boundary claim is made.
