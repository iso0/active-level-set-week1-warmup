# Independent decision: representation helps; regional discovery remains unresolved

20 October 2026. Completed local campaign, with the original deadline of
08:40:14 UTC unchanged. **The defensible contribution is a tested, loss-linked
implementation and a separation of mechanisms, not a generally superior new
acquisition.** The structural model learns the deliberately anisotropic boundary;
the surprise/local policy does not improve the primary aggregate endpoint or
establish query savings. Small islands remain a substantive failure, including
islands observed during startup.

## What was actually run

The preserved v1.1 development study contains 807 trajectories. Its residual
gate never activated in the 42 mixture/regional worlds, so its zero local effect
did not test an active intervention. This motivated one prospective v2 revision:
a decision-contradiction gate and coordinate-anisotropic covariance alternatives.
V2 ran 1,278 development and **10,224 independent-TEST trajectories**, followed
by 84 development and **672 TEST equal-local-share controls**. That is 13,065
completed controlled trajectories, not 13,065 independent realizations. TEST has
**24 realizations in each of 14 fixed four-dimensional families: 336 worlds**.
All arms in a world share initial observations and hidden potential outcomes;
quota controls reuse these worlds. Development and TEST realizations are separate,
but TEST is not evidence about a new function-family distribution or SPH.

Every trajectory reached its registered horizon; there are no failed/incomplete
controlled trajectories. The ledger charges 692,967 controlled fit requests /
4,795,398 component solves, plus 58 / 388 independent reference requests/solves.
These include reconstructions and retained reservations. Software/reference
checks are separate from scientific outcomes. Earlier failed quadrature, the
final exact-check tie error, and interrupted resource-guard attempts are preserved.
No physical simulations, real-data Stage A, C4 or old-pilot rerun occurred.

## The method and the comparison

For a declared primary latent monitor \(f_0\), threshold zero and uniform public
reference distribution \(Q\), the decision is
\(d_t(x)=1\{P(f_0(x)>0\mid D_t)>1/2\}\). Acquisition approximates the expected
decrease in \(L=\int1\{d(x)\ne1[f_0(x)>0]\}\,dQ(x)\), integrating the jointly
paid outputs and missing-channel probabilities. It preserves latent versus
observation uncertainty and within/between-component covariance. Actual paid
updates reweight mixture components; the inexpensive Gaussian-moment lookahead
does not fully reweight hypothetical future components.

The regional policy reserves one quarter of adaptive queries for global random
exploration and uses global SUR otherwise. Two of four slots may instead verify
and investigate a paid sign contradiction, with bounded duration and
corroboration against the frozen pre-anchor posterior. `sur_global` shares the
same model, startup, decision, output contract and global schedule. Thus their
contrast isolates local response within this implementation. The separate
equal-share pair spends exactly 28 of 56 adaptive queries locally in each arm,
isolating surprise-informed anchor choice. Same-selector model comparisons also
change adaptive paths; matched full RANDOM/MAXIMIN paths isolate prediction.
Pure straddle and the literature adaptations are complete-system controls with
their declared schedules, not this same-schedule acquisition contrast.

These are established SUR, Bayesian model averaging and local/global design ideas.
CA-SMART already uses surprise and local investigation; TruVaR and multiscale
GP-LSE address related objectives under different assumptions. Our named
finite-pool adaptations are not reproductions of their continuous-domain or
repeated-sampling guarantees. See [the final mathematical contract](METHOD_AND_ASSUMPTIONS.md)
and [primary-literature audit (RESTRICTED)](../../../docs/research/ARCHIVE_MANIFEST.md#rec-pkg-0223).

## Primary result and the trade-off hidden by the mean

Differences below are final uniform sign error, A minus B; negative favors A.
Intervals are the frozen stratified, paired, whole-world bootstrap intervals.

| Frozen comparison | Difference [95% interval] | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---|
| H1: mixture residual-regional − global SUR | −0.000009 [−0.000058, +0.000041] | Gate active in only 3/336 worlds; 333 identical full paths. |
| H2: mixture − simple, residual-regional selector | +0.000185 [−0.000536, +0.000917] | Proper scores improve, sign error does not. |
| H3: structure boundary-regional − global SUR | −0.000025 [−0.000387, +0.000326] | Gate active in 26/336; 310 identical paths; 109 local queries. |
| H4: structure − simple, boundary-regional selector | −0.032647 [−0.033793, −0.031475] | Representation/system benefit, with important regional harm. |

H4 is the only registered primary pass (Holm p≈0.000400). The sign-flip p-values
require exchangeability/symmetry of paired differences, not merely zero mean;
algorithm identities were not randomized. Keep this qualification alongside
the pass flag. Secondary intervals are pointwise and not simultaneously adjusted.

The anisotropic family explains the broad gain: H4 error falls from **0.4902 to
0.0208** there. Its other 13 families average a **+0.00095** error difference,
a descriptive decomposition, not a replacement endpoint. Matched full-path
structure−simple error differences are −0.01535 [−0.01726,−0.01342] on MAXIMIN
and −0.02327 [−0.02499,−0.02151] on RANDOM. This supports a representation
mechanism beyond changed query paths. It does not show arbitrary nonstationary
or rotated-anisotropic robustness.

Yet H4 island recall falls **0.1317** [0.0799,0.1874], and joint-target
attainment falls from **164/336 to 148/336**. Its restricted query burden
increases **2.607** [1.560,3.631]. Lower mean error and better Brier/log loss
therefore do not establish better discovery or sample efficiency.

**First hit and learning after a hit differ.** Only 1/24 small-unseen worlds
is hit by this system by B64, versus 6/24 for simple/RANDOM; one world has no
island candidate in the finite pool. All 24 small-seen worlds start with an
island observation, but only two receive three island observations and final
recall is .0931. No boundary episode activates in either small-island family.
Startup observations initialize the posterior without retrospective trigger
episodes; the policy does not automatically “adopt” a startup anomaly. Seen and
unseen generators also condition island location differently, so their difference
is not a randomized effect of one extra initial hit.

**Local reaction has limited positive evidence, not a universal null.** Within
the structural model H3 improves four-family island recall by .0276
[.0032,.0578], mostly on larger islands, without a primary-error or query-burden
gain. Equal-share surprise anchoring changes only 18/336 full paths; both arms
spend 9,408 local queries. Its sign-error difference is +.000047
[−.000087,+.000215], island-recall difference +.0123 [.0031,.0241], with slightly
worse proper scores. These are secondary descriptive effects. Forced ungated
local spending is worse than gated/global behavior overall; a gate comparison
is often a comparison of avoiding unnecessary local spending.

**Prediction quality, boundary quality and calibration separate.** H2 improves
Brier by .00450 and log loss by .03081 without sign-error improvement. Structure
has about .994 coverage for its nominal .90 moment-normal latent intervals:
these working intervals are often conservative, not certified calibrated
mixture quantiles. Paired-output versus primary-only query valuation has no
resolved broad error gain (structure: −.00015 [−.00180,+.00151], 72 paired
worlds). Auxiliary RMSE changes are reported separately. Under the same mixture,
SUR improves sign error over VTL by .00241 but worsens log loss by .0130.
Different objectives produce different rankings.

**Corruption remains an information limit.** A spatially coherent monitor bias
can imitate an island. The example figure shows the simple system learning a
spurious contour; the structural system suppresses it in that realization, but
this is not identification of physics. All corruption worlds meet the registered
target early at B12, before later false-positive learning. Two adjacent successful
readouts certify that event, not permanent correctness. All later B64 errors are
retained. Positive collection is also inadequate: small-unseen structural paths
collect 23.6 true positives on average, yet almost never recover the island.

Independent Gaussian/dense covariance references pass, including the WhiteKernel
noise convention. Twelve prespecified TEST paths replay their first eight adaptive
queries with exact IDs and full-posterior hashes. Full integrity checks cover all
12,258 v2/main-quota DEV/TEST paths and 32 active source hashes. All 18 TEST
figures were visually reviewed; [visual review (RESTRICTED)](../../../docs/research/ARCHIVE_MANIFEST.md#rec-pkg-0241)
records qualifications.

The six-prefix finite-mixture quadrature references all meet numerical tolerances,
but moment-SUR selects a different screened winner in **1/6** states. Maximum
score discrepancy is .0026105; regret in the differing case is .0001761.
These are eight screened candidates and 32 reference points per state, not
validation of all full-pool rankings or an exact physical posterior.

No frozen query-savings flag passes. For example structural regional/global
both attain 148/336 and have restricted burden 50.93, versus 164/336 and 48.32
for simple regional. Structural straddle has lower B64 error (.02952), 167/336
attainment and 51.15 burden; it is a strong, much cheaper system control, with
lower island recall (.2426 versus .3009). Its mean full-B64 wall cost is 6.70 s,
versus 51.66 s for structural regional; this is computation, not simulator cost.

contains every arm, censoring, CPU and selection cost. Compute time at target
was not recorded; worker-lifetime peak RSS reaches 765.7 MB. No physical hours
saved or SPH superiority is claimed.

## Independent recommendation and a discriminating next experiment

Keep global SUR and straddle as controls with an appropriate posterior; **do not
promote the regional rule as an acquisition improvement or the coordinate bank
as a universal replacement for Opus's models**. My leading hypothesis is that
the posterior's representation and valuation of a small coherent region are
inadequate even after a paid hit. The strongest competitor is finite-horizon
value: myopic global SUR can rationally skip a first local query whose value
appears only after corroboration. The exact two-step example demonstrates that
possibility, not that this heuristic solves it.

The next discriminating experiment is one bounded, prospective **2×2 at common
paid first-hit prefixes**: existing versus a paid-anchor local-discrepancy
posterior, crossed with one-step versus explicitly two-step loss reduction.
Freeze its local support prior and search cost before new realizations; retain
first-hit acquisition separately, include startup-paid anomalies, and include
noise and coherent-corruption controls. No hidden island/group membership may
enter the selector. If only the model contrast helps under both horizons, that
supports representation; if two-step helps with unchanged calibrated posterior,
that supports horizon value. If neither helps at a paid hit, inspect legitimate
measurement information before another selector sweep. This is a future
proposal, **not an executed extension of TEST**.

| Observed condition | Supported interpretation | Next step justified by it |
|---|---|---|
| Trigger inactive or startup hit has no episode | The local intervention was largely absent; this is not a test of useful active local response everywhere. | Include legitimately paid startup anomalies in a prospectively specified post-hit test. |
| Same-path prediction improves, small-region recall remains poor | Model representation helps the broad contour but does not preserve every local structure. | Test one paid-anchor discrepancy component under the same loss and horizon. |
| A calibrated local first query has little one-step value but enables a useful second query | Finite-horizon information value is possible; the exact toy example establishes existence. | Compare explicit two-step planning with one-step planning on identical prefixes. |
| Noise or coherent corruption produces apparent surprises | Nearby corroboration may reproduce noise structure or monitor bias. | Retain noise controls; physical interpretation needs a legitimate independent channel or stated identification assumption. |
| No first island hit | Post-hit refinement cannot solve the missing observation by itself. | Evaluate first-hit coverage separately and charge exploration; do not provide hidden island locations. |

Opus's already completed RC19 AX also shows no general local advantage; its
strong real-data model and probability improvements deserve separate credit.
The independent [RC19 review (RESTRICTED)](../../../docs/research/ARCHIVE_MANIFEST.md#rec-pkg-0234) identifies upstream
model confounding in physical-feature attribution and censoring limits on query
savings. C3 remains spent; C4/real Stage A remain untouched. Fresh splits of the
inspected simulations cannot supply independent physical confirmation.
