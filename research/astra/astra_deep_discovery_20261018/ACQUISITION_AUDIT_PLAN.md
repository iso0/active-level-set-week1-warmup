# Prospective audit of acquisition approximation on actual paid prefixes

Registered before v2 TEST release. No method, numerical setting or primary
endpoint will be selected using this audit. It is a diagnostic of the approximation
already declared in the design, not another acquisition competitor or new oracle.

Reconstruct the `structure/boundary_regional` posterior at B16 and B40 for the
FIRST registered TEST realization of smooth, island_small_unseen and anisotropic.
Verify its full pre-query snapshot hash against the saved query event. Six fits
(72 components) are charged to the cumulative controlled envelope. Only paid
observations enter the posterior. Read no future outcomes for query valuation.

At each prefix, screen the top five moment-SUR candidates under the full 1024-point
Q cloud and the first three additional unpaid IDs. Compare all eight on the SAME
first 32 Sobol reference points. Compute finite-mixture future risk by independent
one-dimensional adaptive quadrature, reweighting components for every hypothetical
observation. Use both epsabs=1e-7 and 1e-9, at most 400 subintervals, with finite
bounds covering every component mean +/-10 observation standard deviations and
breakpoints at each component mean. The total mixture omitted probability is
below 2 Phi(-10); loss is bounded by 1/2. Record quadrature errors, evaluation
counts, elapsed time, posterior effective component count, score differences,
winner changes, near ties and exact-reference regret on the screened candidates.

Accept the numerical reference only if paired-tolerance score differences <=1e-6
and reported integration errors <=1e-6. Preserve failures and negative gains
beyond tolerance as diagnostics. Do not increase the precision by a scientific
score or pick a new selector. The finite high-precision calculation is not an
exact physical posterior or a guarantee on unscreened candidates/continuum Q.
The 32-reference restriction is intentional: compare approximation to reference
on equal points, not an integration-grid change disguised as mixture error.

This scalar audit complements, rather than replaces, the independent paired
Gaussian covariance checks. A full multivariate mixture lookahead remains an
explicit approximation limitation in the paired-output scientific arms.
