# Completed Astra discovery campaign — results index

Start with [the independent scientific judgment](FINAL_REPORT.md), or the
[plain Turkish explanation](OZET_TR.md). The campaign contains **13,065 completed
controlled trajectories**, including 10,896 TEST trajectories on **336 shared
independent synthetic worlds**. Different arms are not independent realizations.
All 14 families and all failures/interruption records remain available.

The structural covariance alternatives solve the tested coordinate-anisotropy
failure. The local acquisition does not establish aggregate improvement or query
savings; small islands remain poorly learned. This is synthetic mechanism
evidence, not SPH confirmation. The unchanged clock is in [CAMPAIGN.json (RESTRICTED)](../../../docs/research/ARCHIVE_MANIFEST.md#rec-pkg-0032).
