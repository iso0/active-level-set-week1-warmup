# Interpretation clarification — no scientific protocol change

20 October 2026, while TEST runs and before endpoint interpretation.

The additional mixture-aware h1 control restores more than future component
weights. It evaluates the current mixture sign probabilities and, under each
future observation, the component-specific conditional means/variances **and**
their posterior weights. Moment-Gaussian h1/h2 instead evaluate sign risk under
a Gaussian distribution matched to the mixture's moments.

Consequently, a difference between mixture-h1 and moment-h1 is evidence about
the **combined distributional approximation**, not an isolated causal estimate
of component-weight learning alone. A weight-only ablation has not been run and
is not added. The tiny numerical counterexample proves that this approximation
can change an action; it does not prove which subpart explains a scientific
TEST gain. The historical label “component-learning control” identifies the
prospective mechanism motivation, not a stronger attribution than its contrast.

Likewise, the main matched h2-minus-h1 contrast isolates the deployed planning
rule under a common actual model and decision. It does not measure exact
two-step Bayesian value under the finite mixture. Its future second action is
restricted to the current six-point shortlist, with the registered forced
global slot honored; the actual next shortlist is rebuilt after observing the
first paid result. Finite quadrature, finite-resolution tie handling and
replanning remain part of the deployed rule.

Uniform reference-distribution sign error can rationally neglect a low-volume
island. Conditional island recall and regional guards make that loss visible;
they do not insert hidden island membership into the acquisition or guarantee
its preservation. A null result can reflect weak local representation, limited
two-query value, a restrictive shortlist, the loss's weighting, or first-hit
information limits. Use the matched B32 predictions, strong controls, search
paths and numerical fallback counts to distinguish these possibilities as far
as the recorded evidence allows. Do not assert a unique cause from a null mean.

No model, selector, loss, target, seed, threshold, gate or tolerance changes.
The one allowed alternative remains spent; no further experiment is opened.
