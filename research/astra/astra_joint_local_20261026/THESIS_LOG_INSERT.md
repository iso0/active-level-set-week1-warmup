26 October 2026: We tested whether fitting the global background under a local
mixture likelihood, rather than under the null alone, improves early latent
level-set classification. Composite global/local learning is established
([Ba & Joseph,2012 (PENDING_ARCHIVE)](../../../docs/research/ARCHIVE_MANIFEST.md#pending-97d2fbbaa77a62cd);
[Davis,Hans & Santner (PENDING_ARCHIVE)](../../../docs/research/ARCHIVE_MANIFEST.md#pending-35b29b53589c56bc)); this experiment isolates
the learning objective for a constrained rank-one location mixture. Following
32 previously inspected development worlds, an unchanged empirical-Bayes model
was evaluated in144 new synthetic worlds across12 families with two dependent
matched paid paths/world. In72 local-structure worlds its mean BA-AULC B8–24
increment was+.007365 over an extra-optimized global GP and+.004022 over the same
local mixture at fixed null-fitted parameters. The latter missed the frozen
.005 practical criterion despite a positive adjusted one-sided lower bound.
Regional harms and poor island attainment remained; no acquisition trial was
opened. Thus joint fitting partially addresses the prior limitation, but does
not establish reliable boundary recovery or query savings. Evidence is confined
to synthetic latent-monitor sign, not recorded Keyhole or physical validation.
All outcomes, raw predictions, hashes, numerical checks, failures and costs are
preserved in astra_joint_local_20261026/RESULTS_INDEX.json.
